# Garrison DAT evidence

## Evidence boundary

Pinned `genie-dat` exposes `StaticUnitTypeAttributes.garrison_capacity` and the
building fields `garrison_type`, `garrison_heal_rate`, and
`garrison_repair_rate`. The project JSON extractor previously omitted all four.
It now preserves them, and
`tools/dat_metadata/generate_garrison_metadata.py` selects represented Town
Center 109, Castle 82, Watch Tower 79, Guard Tower 234, Keep 235, and Bombard
Tower 236 records.

`generated/garrison_dat_metadata.json` was regenerated from the supplied legal
VER 5.7 DAT at
`/tmp/aoe-assets.TNMZJ0/app/Data/empires2_x1_p1.dat`. It records every requested
building. The alternate supplied DAT at
`/tmp/aoe-assets.grSVdf/app/Data/empires2_x1_p1.dat` has the same SHA-256,
`e49d05b326ecf4a14e0cddd5171718c6849abe2548939bb9a93a8f3039753d9d`,
and independently generated byte-identical bounded JSON. The TNMZJ0 copy is
the committed fixture source.

Regenerate from a legally obtained VER 5.7 DAT:

```sh
cargo run --manifest-path tools/dat_metadata/Cargo.toml --release -- \
  /path/to/empires2_x1_p1.dat > /tmp/aoe-metadata.json
python3 tools/dat_metadata/generate_garrison_metadata.py \
  /tmp/aoe-metadata.json
```

The generator fails rather than emitting partial evidence when a required
building or garrison field is absent.

## Capacity and accepted classes

`garrison_capacity` is a direct numeric DAT field. `garrison_type` is preserved
as a raw signed byte. The pinned library does not name or decode its bits. It
therefore does **not** prove
accepted unit classes, ownership/alliance rules, whether one unit consumes one
slot, or technology/civilization capacity modifiers.

Do not translate a raw type value into “Villagers,” “infantry,” “archers,”
“cavalry,” or another acceptance list without independent format or runtime
evidence.

## Runtime-unknown behavior

The following require original-runtime validation even after raw values are
regenerated:

- accepted-unit class and ownership interpretation;
- capacity upgrades, civilization modifiers, and slot cost per unit;
- entry range, pathing, queueing, cancellation, and full-building behavior;
- ejection placement and behavior on conversion, deletion, or destruction;
- healing and repair-rate units, cadence, eligibility, and rounding;
- projectile count or attack contribution from occupants;
- target selection, minimum-range interaction, accuracy, and missed shots;
- whether zero area range/blast level means strictly single-target damage;
- friendly-fire, allied/enemy filtering, armor-class resolution, and elevation.

Reconstruction tests may define bounded behavior for these items, but those
tests do not convert it into commercial DAT or runtime evidence.

## Represented runtime contract

Capacity uses the decoded numeric fields exactly: Town Center 15, Castle 20,
and represented tower line 5. One accepted unit consumes one slot. Buildings
must be living, completed, owned by the unit, and below capacity. Castle
accepts represented non-ship, non-siege human military and Villagers. Town
Center and towers accept Villagers, Monks, infantry, and non-cavalry archers.
Animals, Relics, Trade Carts, ships, siege, enemies, and allies are rejected.
These class and ownership lists are reconstruction policy because raw
`garrison_type` bits remain undecoded.

Entry uses normal pathing to the building perimeter. On arrival, unit orders
clear and position becomes shelter origin. Ungarrison scans perimeter tiles in
stable clockwise edge order and releases occupants in entity order, never
placing two units on one tile. Explicit deletion now applies this same ejection
to every represented garrison building, not only Town Centers. Destruction
also attempts deterministic ejection before rubble removal.

Represented healing interprets raw 0.1/0.2 values as HP per source second:
Town Center/tower occupants gain one HP every 50 simulation ticks and Castle
occupants every 25. Herbal Medicine quarters those intervals. DAT does not
prove this conversion, rounding, or Herbal Medicine cadence.

Transport Ships are a separate garrison domain. Capacity is 5, 10 with
Careening, and 15 with Dry Dock. Embark requires same ownership, adjacency,
available capacity, and a non-ship passenger. Disembark uses deterministic
connected walkable landing tiles. Transport destruction or explicit deletion
kills/removes all passengers; no commercial ejection behavior is claimed.

Save validation rejects missing shelters, dead shelters, invalid accepted
classes, wrong owners, wrong stored positions, and capacity overflow. Existing
save/scenario/replay tests cover restored garrisons and Ungarrison commands.
Adversarial tests additionally cover distinct multi-unit ejection, Castle
deletion, transport loss, and projectile contribution.

### Defensive-volley helper boundary

`garrison_volley_projectile_count` is now the pure, shared projectile-count
policy used by runtime and focused tests. It preserves the represented caps:
Town Center 10 total arrows and no ungarrisoned baseline, Castle 20 total,
Watch/Guard/Keep 5 total, and Bombard Tower one cannon projectile unaffected
by occupants. Only Villagers and archer-class occupants enter its contributing
count. Save/reload executes the same helper from restored occupant state.

The pinned DAT proves each shelter's weapon class, minimum/maximum range,
reload, accuracy, and projectile ID, but contains no occupant-to-projectile
formula. The pinned executable corpus has not yielded a bounded decompilation
that connects its garrison list to projectile multiplicity. Therefore the
helper's caps and per-occupant contribution remain explicitly reconstruction
policy, not a claim of commercial equivalence. In particular, unit-DPS
weighting, secondary-projectile damage, Teuton capacity/fire modifiers, and
occupant effects on Bombard Towers remain unproved.
